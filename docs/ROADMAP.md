# Roadmap

This document describes the long-term direction of Budget Brewer.

Unlike `TODO.md`, the roadmap is not intended to capture every task or bug.
Instead, it documents larger milestones and product goals.

Features should move from this document into `TODO.md` when active
development begins.

---

# Vision

Budget Brewer is a privacy-first budgeting journal focused on helping users
record, organize, understand, and retain ownership of their financial data.

Rather than recommending financial decisions, Budget Brewer provides clear,
transparent tools that help users understand their own financial history and
build confidence in managing it.

The app should remain approachable for beginners while providing enough depth
to support long-term financial organization.
